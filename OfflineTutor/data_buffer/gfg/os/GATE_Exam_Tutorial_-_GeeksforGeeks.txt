# GATE Exam Tutorial - GeeksforGeeks

The Graduate Aptitude Test in Engineering (GATE) is a national-level exam in India, jointly conducted by the Indian Institute of Science (IISc) and seven Indian Institutes of Technology (IITs) on a rotational basis. The exam serves as a gateway for admissions to postgraduate programmes and recruitment in public sector undertakings (PSUs).This tutorial contains detailed sources for GATE CS & IT and GATE DA preparation.
